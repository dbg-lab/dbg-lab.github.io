### Principal Investigator

{{< profile-card 
    image="/images/daniel-goodman.png"

Dr. Goodman is an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Cancer Biology](https://www.med.upenn.edu/cbio/) and an Investigator at the [Center for Cellular Immunotherapies](https://www.med.upenn.edu/cci/) and the [Parker Institute for Cancer Immunotherapy](https://www.parkerici.org/) at the University of Pennsylvania. He earned his Ph.D. in Medical Engineering and Physics from the [HST Program](https://hst.mit.edu/) at MIT, where he worked with [George Church](https://wyss.harvard.edu/team/core-faculty/george-church/) on high-throughput design and measurement of microbial genomes and genetic elements. He then joined UCSF as a Jane Coffin Childs Postdoctoral Fellow, where he trained in the laboratories of [Kole Roybal](https://roybal.ucsf.edu/) and [Alexander Marson](https://marsonlab.ucsf.edu/), applying these high-throughput synthetic approaches to T cell engineering and CAR-T receptor design.
